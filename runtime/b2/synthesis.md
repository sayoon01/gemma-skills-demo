# B2 Final Synthesis Contract

## Role

Write the final research report using only the validated Evidence Pack.

Do not perform a new research phase during synthesis.

Do not introduce information that is not present in the Evidence Pack.

## Citation integrity

Every factual citation must point to a VERIFIED source contained in the Evidence Pack.

Do not invent:

- URLs;
- publishers;
- page numbers;
- titles;
- dates;
- source tiers.

## Claim handling

### Triangulated

If a material claim has at least two independent verified sources, present it normally with the relevant citations.

### Single-source

If a useful material claim has only one verified source, mark it clearly as single-source.

### Unsupported

If requested information has no verified evidence, state that evidence was insufficient or that the information was not publicly confirmed.

### Conflicting

If credible verified sources disagree:

- explain the disagreement;
- cite both sides;
- do not silently choose one unless evidence quality clearly justifies doing so.

## Facts and analysis

Clearly distinguish:

- verified factual findings;
- analytical interpretation;
- implications or recommendations.

## Gaps

Include unresolved research gaps and the reason each remains unresolved.

## Sources

Include only sources present in the validated Evidence Pack.

Do not include search-only sources.

## Efficiency

Use compact Evidence Pack content.

Do not request raw worker conversation history or full raw webpages during final synthesis.
